

\# DIGITAL SKILL CONNECTION

\### Connecting Skills to Opportunities



\## 1. Project Overview



Digital Skill Connection is a digital services marketplace designed to connect customers with skilled workers across Rwanda. The platform will be accessible through a website and a mobile-friendly interface.



It allows customers to find trusted service providers while helping skilled workers reach more customers and access employment opportunities.



\## 2. Problem Statement



Many skilled workers in Rwanda struggle to find customers and employment opportunities. At the same time, customers often face difficulties finding reliable and qualified people to provide services.



Digital Skill Connection aims to address these challenges through one accessible digital platform.



\## 3. Project Aim and Objectives



The main aim is to create a platform that connects customers with skilled workers across Rwanda.



The objectives are to:

\- Reduce unemployment by creating more opportunities for skilled workers.

\- Help customers find reliable service providers.

\- Make it easier to search, compare, and request services.

\- Improve trust through worker verification and customer reviews.

\- Support secure payment processing.



\## 4. Business Process Actors



The process involves five main actors:



1\. \*\*Customer:\*\* Searches for services, selects workers, submits requests, makes payments, and provides feedback.

2\. \*\*Skilled Worker:\*\* Registers, submits skills and personal information, accepts job requests, and provides services.

3\. \*\*Administrator:\*\* Verifies worker profiles and handles customer complaints.

4\. \*\*Digital Skill Connection Platform:\*\* Displays verified workers, manages requests, records transactions, calculates commissions, and stores feedback.

5\. \*\*Payment Service:\*\* Processes payments and transfers the remaining payment to workers after commission deduction.



\## 5. Business Process Description



The process begins when a skilled worker registers and submits personal details and skills for verification. The administrator reviews the information and approves eligible workers.



A customer searches for a service and views available verified workers. The customer selects a preferred worker and submits a job request. The worker accepts or rejects the request. If accepted, both parties agree on the price and schedule.



The worker performs the service, and the customer makes payment through the payment service. After successful payment, the platform records the transaction and deducts its commission. The remaining payment is transferred to the worker.



Finally, the customer confirms satisfaction and submits a rating or complaint. Complaints are handled by the administrator.



\## 6. Data Management Activities



The business process involves the following data operations:



\- \*\*CREATE:\*\* Creating customer accounts, worker profiles, job requests, payment records, and customer reviews.

\- \*\*UPDATE:\*\* Updating profile information, worker availability, job status, payment status, and worker ratings.

\- \*\*RETRIEVE:\*\* Searching for services, viewing worker profiles, checking job requests, and consulting transaction records.

\- \*\*ARCHIVE:\*\* Keeping completed job records, payment history, and complaint records for future reference.



\## 7. Expected Benefits



\- Increased employment opportunities for skilled workers.

\- Easier access to services across Rwanda.

\- Improved trust between customers and service providers.

\- Better management of service requests and payments.

\- Increased access to customers for skilled workers.
